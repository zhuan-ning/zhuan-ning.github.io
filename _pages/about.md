My name is Zhuan Ning (宁专). I was born in Yiyang, Hunan Province, China. I am currently a PhD candidate at the [University of Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://www.ucas.ac.cn/) and [Institute of Theoretical Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences](http://www.itp.cas.cn/), supervised by [Prof. Rong-Gen Cai](https://itp.cas.cn/sourcedb/zw/zjrck/lrsz/202312/t20231227_6931054.html). Before I started my PhD, I was a Master's and an undergraduate student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. I work in gravity and cosmology. My research interests include numerical relativity, black hole dynamics, primordial black holes, and gravitational wave physics.
